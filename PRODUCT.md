# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students or individuals interested in generating data visualizations. They use the tool in an academic or analytical context where they need quick, accurate visual insights from their datasets.

## Product Purpose
To allow users to upload a dataset and request visualizations via natural language queries. Success means the platform generates the correct visualization, and the user can easily refine or modify it through an ongoing conversational chat interface.

## Brand Personality
Professional, Balanced, Approachable. It strikes a perfect blend—not overly sparse or minimalistic, but retaining enough structure to feel like a serious analytical tool without being overwhelming.

## Anti-references
- Overly crowded or dense dashboards (e.g., traditional enterprise BI tools).
- Interfaces that dump too much information on the screen at once.

## Design Principles
- **Focus on the Conversation**: The chat interface is the primary driver of changes; keep it central and clear.
- **Clarity over Density**: Provide enough information to be professional, but avoid crowding the screen.
- **Immediate Visual Feedback**: Ensure the generated visualization is the hero of the screen, updating seamlessly with user queries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific requirements beyond standard best practices.
